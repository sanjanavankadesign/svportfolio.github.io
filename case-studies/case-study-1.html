01. Overview

What is Connect?

Connect is an existing B2B in-house product that builds recruiter-candidate relationships by optimizing communication flows.

FIG 02: Connect Platform Interface & Core Feature Architecture Overview lock Protected under NDA — Click to view info

Business Brief:

Redesign Connect to enable enhanced communication and peer collaboration between freelance recruiters, SMBs, and candidates.

Why Connect x Freelance Recruiter?

Freelance recruiters and SMBs represent a fragmented, underserved segment of the $690B+ recruiting market — one that lacks purpose-built infrastructure designed for their specific workflows, which still operate through email, spreadsheets, and disconnected tools as they cannot afford expensive enterprise software.

From Insight to Decision

What we learned, and what it changed

Every core feature traces back to something we saw or heard in research.

Insight · Shadowing

Recruiters constantly switched between email, Excel, WhatsApp, and candidate logs just to track a single hire.

Decision

Bring every pipeline into one dashboard so status is visible at a glance, without switching tools.

Feature 01 →
Insight · Candidate Interviews

Over 80% of the ghosting candidates described traced back to broken, manual email chains.

Decision

Merge SMS, email, and phone into one conversation timeline, so no thread with a candidate gets lost.

Feature 02 →
Insight · Recruiter Interviews

Time spent screening resumes and maintaining spreadsheets competed directly with time spent closing roles.

Decision

Rank candidates by match score and make outreach a batch action instead of one message at a time.

Feature 03 →
Insight · Workflow Mapping

Recruiters already had role briefs in docs and messages, yet traditional ATS tools made them re-enter everything into long forms.

Decision

Replace the form with a conversational builder: paste a brief, dictate it, or start from a quick action.

06. Roadmap

Way Forward & Next Steps

fork_right

Implement Edge Cases

During the iteration process we found a lot of edge cases which we plan to implement in the next phase of development.

pie_chart

Develop Segments & Reports

While we have a basic idea for what we want to do with segments and reports, we need to build upon it to develop it further.

Scope & Constraints

  • Concept stage: the project concluded at high fidelity and was not taken into development.
  • Deliberately deferred: Segments, Reports, and the edge cases found during iteration were scoped to a later phase to keep the core recruiter flow focused.
  • Client NDA: final screens are blurred here, so this case study leans on process and rationale.

How Success Would Be Measured

  • Candidate response rate and a drop in reported ghosting.
  • Time to first contact after a candidate is shortlisted.
  • Tools per hire: fewer switches between email, sheets, and chat.
