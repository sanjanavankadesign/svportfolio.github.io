01. Understanding the Challenge ✦

The Problem Statement

Users experienced cognitive overload during checkout and onboarding. Key data indicated a high drop-off rate due to unclear navigation, inconsistent visual hierarchy, and lengthy multi-step forms.

Core UX Goals:

  • Simplify complex workflows into clear, progressive disclosure steps.
  • Establish an accessible, modern design system to ensure UI consistency.
  • Reduce user error rates and increase speed-to-completion by at least 30%.
02. Discovery Phase ✦

User Research & Key Insights

I conducted 12 qualitative user interviews and analyzed session recordings. Three core pain points emerged:

warning

Information Overload

Too much text presented at once without visual grouping, causing users to overlook essential actions.

help

Unclear Hierarchy

Primary buttons blended in with secondary actions, leading to misclicks and confusion.

05. Reflection ✦

Key Takeaways & Retrospective

1. Test early with low-fidelity prototypes

Validating concept flows early saved weeks of visual redesign efforts later in the process.

2. Design tokens accelerate implementation

Working closely with developers using shared design tokens resulted in pixel-perfect production builds.
