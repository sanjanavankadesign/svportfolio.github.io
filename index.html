Process

How I Work

A human-centered practice, augmented by AI. Research and judgment stay firmly with me; AI extends how broadly I explore and how quickly I move from concept to prototype.

01

Discover

Conducting user interviews and contextual inquiry to build a grounded understanding of needs, behaviors, and pain points.

Human-led: every interview conducted and synthesized first-hand.

02

Define

Every project begins on paper: articulating the problem, mapping flows, and sketching screens to establish structure before pixels.

With AI: pressure-testing assumptions and uncovering edge cases with Claude.

03

Craft

Translating sketches into high-fidelity interfaces, cohesive visual systems, and considered interactions in Figma.

With AI: broadening exploration with Claude and Figma before converging on a direction.

04

Test & Ship

Testing prototypes with users where the timeline allows, and refining flows until they are clear and ready to hand off.

With AI: building functional prototypes in Antigravity to test real interactions sooner.

Still evolving This approach took shape after a conversation with designer Harshit Taak on the future of UX in the age of AI. I continue to explore how AI can extend my practice, while keeping research, judgment, and empathy firmly human.
